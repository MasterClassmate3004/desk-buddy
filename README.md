This is a desk study buddy!

Built for company while long study sessions right on your desk!

It includes 4 screen pages, has an in-built Pomodoro time tracker for increased focus during sessions, battery level indication and it also blinks.

The 3D case is nearly a cube and less than 10cm in each dimension. It is small, doesn't use up too much space on your desk so that you can read more books at once!
